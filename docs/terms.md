# Terms of service template — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

**Draft only; not an offer or binding service terms.**
Complete with counsel and the product owner before onboarding paying customers.

## Parties and service

Identify legal entity, contact/address, effective date, eligibility, contracting
authority and governing jurisdiction. Describe the actual hosted service and
separate it from locally run source and the legacy public demo.

BlastRadius provides static modeled change-impact findings. It does not warrant
complete AWS coverage, exploitability assessment, prevention of incidents or
safety of a deployment. Customers retain responsibility for infrastructure
decisions and independent review. Define any contractual warranty carefully.

## Customer responsibilities

Require authorization to submit infrastructure/repository data and sufficient
rights to process it. Define acceptable use, account security, membership
administration, prohibited abuse and fair-use limits.
Do not authorize attacks against third parties or attempts to access other tenants.

Specify customer data ownership, limited processing rights and export/deletion
process. Do not claim ownership of customers' Terraform. Identify actual software
licensing separately; the baseline has no standalone license grant.

## Commercial and liability provisions to approve

Complete pricing/currency/taxes, renewal/cancellation, refunds, trial behavior,
usage limits and service suspension through [billing terms](billing-terms.md).
Choose any support/SLA commitments only after operations can meet them.

Counsel must review warranties, liability limits, indemnities, termination,
dispute resolution, governing law and statutory rights. Placeholder disclaimers
are not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific review.

Define notice and consent for material changes, termination export/deletion
windows and relationship to the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
Do not publish this template as an approved commercial contract.
