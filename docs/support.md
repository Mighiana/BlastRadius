# Support and contact — LEGAL REVIEW TEMPLATE

For non-sensitive bugs and product questions, use
[repository issues](https://github.com/Mighiana/BlastRadius/issues).
Share revision, local/hosted context, minimal synthetic reproduction and redacted
logs. Do not upload real Terraform plans, tokens or customer identifiers.

For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
If private reporting is unavailable, request a private channel without exposing
details publicly. No support email, domain or private channel is invented here.

## Before a commercial launch

The owner must publish a verified support contact, legal entity, service hours,
supported versions, escalation process and any contracted response targets.
Choose whether community and paid support differ. Verify these commitments
against actual staffing/operations; no response-time guarantee exists in this draft.

Privacy requests, billing disputes and urgent security reports need appropriate
private routes and identity verification. Do not ask users to post them publicly.
Approve this page together with [privacy](privacy.md), [terms](terms.md) and
[billing terms](billing-terms.md).
